Purpose & Ambition

Built on Engineering Rigor & Domain Passion

We founded AethercodeTech with one resolute commitment: to democratize high-end travel software infrastructure so that every travel agency, tour operator, and corporate TMC can operate with the same technological superiority as billion-dollar OTAs.

Our Mission

To eliminate technical friction and prohibitive entry barriers in global travel commerce by delivering plug-and-play B2B/B2C portals, robust API normalization engines, and turnkey white-label booking software. We make complex GDS, LCC, and hotel bedbank protocols effortlessly accessible to all.

  • 100% transparent pricing and low barrier to entry
  • Unified JSON responses replacing legacy SOAP/XML
  • Rapid time-to-market with 7-day portal rollouts

Our Vision

To become the world's most dependable and developer-friendly travel technology backbone—orchestrating billions of flight, hotel, visa, and vacation bookings annually across 50+ countries with sub-second response times and bulletproof 99.99% availability.

  • Global multi-currency and multi-language support
  • Next-gen AI fare forecasting and dynamic margin engines
  • ISO 27001 & PCI-DSS certified zero-trust architecture

Enterprise Stack

Built on Resilient, High-Throughput Cloud Tech

A look under the hood at the engineering choices that make AethercodeTech systems ultra-fast, fault-tolerant, and secure.

Multi-Provider Aggregation

Queries up to 12 inventory sources simultaneously (Amadeus, Sabre, TBO, Tripjack, Hotelbeds) and removes duplicate itineraries with intelligent sorting.

Sub-Millisecond Redis Caching

Multi-layer distributed caching reduces GDS query costs and delivers instant availability response times to high-volume OTA traffic.

Kubernetes Microservices

Containerized microservices auto-scale during peak holiday booking seasons, ensuring zero downtime and 99.99% uptime compliance.

Real-Time Ledger Engine

Double-entry financial ledger for agent credit limits, top-ups, transaction markups, refund voiding, and automated GST reporting.

PCI-DSS Level 1 Security

End-to-end tokenization and bank-grade encryption guarantee that customer credit card data is handled strictly per global security standards.

Omnichannel Booking APIs

Single backend powers desktop web, responsive mobile web, native Flutter iOS/Android apps, and third-party corporate integrations.
